About Me

Turning Data into Business Impact.

I am a Business Analyst specializing in banking analytics, business intelligence, and data-driven decision-making, with 28 months of professional experience and an MBA in Business Analytics from Vijay Patil School of Management, DY Patil University.

My work combines SQL, Advanced Excel, Power BI, ETL workflows, and KPI reporting to transform business data into actionable insights. Across my experience with HDFC Bank and Kotak Bank, I contributed to measurable improvements, including a 25% reduction in data processing time, campaigns associated with a 6–9% daily conversion uplift, and an approximately 8% improvement in model performance.

Beyond traditional analytics, I explored AI-driven business solutions during my HDFC Bank summer internship, working on an AI-based customer verification project and an advertising script generator. This combination of banking domain knowledge, analytical problem-solving, and exposure to AI helps me approach business challenges with both a data-focused and business-oriented perspective.

My goal is simple: turn complex data into clear insights, efficient processes, and better business decisions.
